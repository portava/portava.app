**generated** `uniffi/openmls/openmls.kt`, every one a JNA symbol
(`Structure` ×52, `sun` ×23, `Pointer` ×8, `Library`, `Native`, `ByReference`…).

**Confirms three earlier commits empirically:** the NDK linker wiring
(`fbb9e6db0`, which was labelled PREDICTED — now observed correct), the ABI
staging (`23a629080`), and the `preBuild.dependsOn` wiring (`43e4d1ef7`).
