# 07 — Creator Economy

## 1. Principle

Reward verified travel value, not attention alone.

Do not pay directly for:
- views,
- likes,
- follower count,
- raw comments.

Those can be supporting evidence but should not define payout.

## 2. Creator value types

### Discovery Creator
Finds valuable places early.

### Trail Builder
Creates/maintains useful Trails.

### Local Expert
Consistently contributes accurate destination knowledge.

### Itinerary Creator
Builds plans that travelers actually use.

### Experience Host
Hosts paid real-world experiences.

### Travel Partner
Provides approved marketplace services.

## 3. Traveler Impact

Internal concept measuring outcomes such as:
- save-to-trip,
- itinerary adoption,
- verified booking,
- verified visit,
- route completion,
- repeat use,
- post-visit confirmation.

Do not expose one raw public score that creators can game.

## 4. Earnings eligibility

Suggested progression:
- Explorer
- Contributor
- Trusted Contributor
- Trail Builder / Local Expert
- Monetization Eligible
- Creator Partner

Eligibility can consider:
- account age,
- verification,
- trust/safety,
- contribution history,
- fraud signals,
- payout compliance.

## 5. Discovery royalties

Long-term concept:
Creators may continue receiving attribution when durable content continues to generate verified economic value.

Do not promise perpetual revenue before unit economics are proven.

## 6. Revenue share sources

- booking commission,
- marketplace fee,
- affiliate commission,
- paid Trail/guide product,
- sponsored collaboration where explicitly attributed.

## 7. Multi-party attribution

A conversion may involve:
- Trail builder,
- original content creator,
- place page,
- later recommender,
- Portava ranking.

The attribution engine should record contributions before deciding payout weights.

## 8. No payout formula hard-coding

Store:
- gross revenue,
- attribution candidates,
- confidence,
- rule version,
- provisional share.

Actual percentages must remain configurable.

## 9. Fraud controls

Detect:
- self-booking loops,
- collusive groups,
- synthetic accounts,
- paid engagement,
- fake visits,
- refund abuse,
- circular transactions.

## 10. Acceptance criteria

Creator economy infrastructure is ready when:
- value can be attributed,
- earnings can be recorded without paying,
- rules are versioned,
- fraud holds exist,
- historical recalculation is possible.
