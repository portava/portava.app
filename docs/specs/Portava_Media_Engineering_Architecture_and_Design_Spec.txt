
PORTAVA
Media Engineering Architecture& Design Specification
Media v2 — Discovery + Live Intelligence Network
Canonical developer specification
“See the world. Understand it. Experience it.”
Product rule
Portava Media is the visual layer of the real world. It lets users see what places, experiences, people, and hidden gems are like now; understand what is changing; act on what they see; contribute back to the intelligence network; and preserve what happened afterward.
Design constraint
Media must not visually or behaviorally resemble Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube. The organizing hierarchy is World → Experience → Place → Time → People → Media, not Creator → Post → Engagement.

2. Non-Negotiable Product Principles
World-first, not creator-first.
No infinite-feed dependency.
Media does not own truth; Live Intelligence owns current claims and confidence.
A photo or video may become an evidence candidate but never becomes truth automatically.
Observed, inferred, user-claimed, generated, and predicted information remain distinguishable.
Every meaningful media object connects to action or context.
Authentic media outranks generated fallback media.
Privacy, location precision, blocks, trust, and eligibility resolve before client projection.
Hidden Gems are first-class and require stronger protection than normal Places.
Success is measured by useful real-world outcomes, not merely minutes watched.

16.2 Gem Protection
Exact location may remain hidden until deliberate open.
Approximate area only for sensitive/fragile locations.
Sensitive natural-site and protected-place rules.
Capacity and overcrowding awareness.
No popularity-first ranking.
Paid promotion never increases factual confidence.
Access restrictions and seasonality.
Suppress aggressive recommendations if a small place is being overloaded.

17. Time Architecture
EARLIER ───────── ● ─────── NOW ───────── LATER
Support Earlier, Now, Typical, and Likely Next. Historical and forecast states must be visually distinct and forecasts carry confidence.

46. Visual Design Specification
Dark/night-friendly primary foundation with high contrast and clear state labels.
Large place/world imagery and spatial mosaics instead of repeated stacked cards.
Geographic context and Map integration.
Time rails and temporal state as first-class UI.
Subtle current-state pulses; no fake-live treatment.
Minimal visible vanity metrics.
Clear freshness/confidence language.
Observed, inferred, and predicted states use distinct visual treatments.
Hidden Gems use distinct discovery/protection visual language.
Compass is a primary intelligence/action control.
Creator identity is visible but secondary in world-first lenses.
46.1 Hidden Gem Visual Language
Gem icon or geometric discovery marker
Subtle edge glow or contour treatment
Map discovery contour / approximate zone treatment
Recently Confirmed / Worth the Detour / Still Hidden / Seasonal labels
Avoid Viral / Trending / Hot / popularity-counter language
46.2 Anti-Patterns
TikTok-style endless vertical video feed
Instagram-style creator-first stacked posts
YouTube thumbnail/title/channel list
Facebook-style newsfeed cards
Follower/view counts dominating Media
Heart/Like as primary hierarchy
Autoplay as primary navigation
Random engagement-ranked viral feed
Full-screen stranger video immediately on Media open
