PHASE 6 — Booking request flow

Create full booking flow.

Traveler selects:
- city
- category
- date
- start time
- duration
- group size
- meeting area
- public meeting preference
- notes
- language preference
- budget
- deposit or full payment option, depending on configuration

Booking statuses:
- draft
- requested
- accepted
- declined
- expired
- cancelled_by_traveler
- cancelled_by_buddy
- paid_deposit
- paid_full
- confirmed
- in_progress
- completed
- disputed
- refunded
- no_show
- closed

Flow:
1. Traveler sends request.
2. Buddy receives request.
3. Buddy accepts or declines.
4. If accepted, traveler pays deposit or full amount.
5. Booking becomes confirmed.
6. Telegraph chat opens or unlocks for both sides.
7. App sends reminders.
8. Booking can be started/check-in.
9. Booking can be completed.
10. Traveler and buddy can review each other.
11. Disputes/reporting available.

Do not allow confirmed booking without required payment state unless admin config allows manual/free bookings.
