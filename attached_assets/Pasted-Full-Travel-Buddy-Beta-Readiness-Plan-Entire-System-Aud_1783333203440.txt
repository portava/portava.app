Full Travel Buddy Beta Readiness Plan — Entire System Audit

Mission:

Bring the entire Travel Buddy app into full beta testing mode for a few hundred real users. This includes Passport, Pulse, Discovery, Create, Trips, Events, Circles, Rent a Buddy, Telegraph, Compass AI, Find Your Circle, Trip Crew, Maps, Safety, Notifications, Settings, onboarding, admin/support tools, database wiring, algorithms, and all UI actions.

This is not a rebuild. This is an audit-first, fix-only, production-beta hardening pass. Every function in the app must be reviewed, wired, tested, and either marked beta-ready or honestly limited with a visible reason.

No visible app function should be decorative, fake, dead-ended, or disconnected from the backend.

Core rule:

Every button must do one of three things:

1. Complete the real intended action.
2. Open the correct real screen/modal.
3. Be hidden or disabled with an honest beta explanation.

No silent failures. No fake success. No placeholder flows. No broken CTAs.

---

For this beta, Travel Buddy must support the full essential product:

Travel Buddy helps users create a travel Passport, discover people/events/trips/circles, create plans, join experiences, rent a local buddy, message travelers, coordinate safely, and use recommendations based on real app data and privacy settings.
