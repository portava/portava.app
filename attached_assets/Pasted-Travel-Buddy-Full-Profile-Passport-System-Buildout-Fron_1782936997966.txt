Travel Buddy — Full Profile / Passport System Buildout, Frontend + Backend

You are building the full Travel Buddy user profile system.

Important product direction:
Travel Buddy is a social media app for travelers. The profile is not just an account page. It is the user’s Passport — their public travel identity, trust layer, social graph, activity hub, travel history, badges, stamps, posts, trips, circles, and optional Rent a Buddy provider profile.

Do not delete existing working systems.
Do not fake users, posts, stamps, reviews, trips, bookings, or activity.
Inspect what exists first.
Patch/build only after inventory.
Preserve auth/session behavior.
Use real backend data.
Everything must be cohesive with Pulse, Discovery, Trips, Telegraph, Rent a Buddy, Trust Score, verification, delayed posting, privacy, and safety.
