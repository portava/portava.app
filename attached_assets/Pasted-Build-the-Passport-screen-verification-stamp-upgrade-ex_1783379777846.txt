1. Who the traveler is.
2. Where they are from.
3. Their trust score.
4. Their official Passport Verified stamp.
5. Their travel stamps.
6. Their postcards.
7. Their verification levels.

It should feel premium, safe, social, and travel-native — like a digital passport mixed with a modern travel social profile.
