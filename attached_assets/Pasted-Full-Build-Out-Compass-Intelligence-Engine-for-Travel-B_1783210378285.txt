Full Build-Out: Compass Intelligence Engine for Travel Buddy

Goal:
Build Compass as Travel Buddy’s app-wide intelligence engine. Compass should power personalized discovery, smart search, event/trip/place recommendations, buddy matching, itinerary suggestions, Telegraph recommendations, safety-aware suggestions, Pulse/Postcard ranking, and contextual travel guidance.

Compass must not be a fake chatbot layer. Compass must use real backend/database truth, respect privacy, and connect to existing Travel Buddy systems.

Core Compass rules:
- No fake recommendations.
- No hardcoded demo content.
- No local-only intelligence truth.
- Compass must use real backend/database data.
- Compass must respect privacy, blocked users, muted users, invite-only access, age restrictions, verification gates, trust gates, moderation, and location privacy.
- Compass should explain why it recommends something when useful.
- Compass should never expose private user data.
- Compass should never reveal exact live location unless the user intentionally shares it and app policy allows it.
- Compass should work across Discovery, Search, Trips, Events, Passport, Telegraph, Pulse, Rent a Buddy, Hidden Gems, Places, City pages, and Safety.
- Build in phases.
- Audit first, then patch only confirmed gaps.
- Keep Compass V1 deterministic, privacy-safe, explainable, and connected to real app data.
- AI can enhance intent/explanations later, but database truth controls eligibility, access, ranking candidates, and actions.
