Finish Travel Buddy Rent a Buddy End-to-End

Goal:
Finish Rent a Buddy as a real, backend-wired, safety-first marketplace inside Travel Buddy.

Rent a Buddy lets travelers book verified locals or experienced travelers for safe travel help, including:
- city help
- social exploration
- language support
- nightlife guidance
- shopping help
- arrival support
- food / hidden-gem help
- content / photo / video help
- itinerary help
- trip or event support
- local logistics

Core boundary:
Rent a Buddy is not dating, not escorting, not adult services, not off-app cash arrangements, and not private unmoderated meetups.

Use real database/backend truth only. Do not fake:
- buddies
- availability
- bookings
- prices
- reviews
- verification
- payouts
- payments
- ratings
- profile status
- booking status
- Telegraph threads
- admin approval

Do not rebuild unrelated systems.
