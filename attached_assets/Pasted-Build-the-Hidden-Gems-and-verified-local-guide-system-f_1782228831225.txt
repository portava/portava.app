CORE PRINCIPLE

Hidden Gems should help travelers discover authentic, useful, safer, and locally recommended places without exposing private user locations or sensitive places.

Hidden Gems can include:
- Food spots

- photo spots

- viewpoints
- markets
- nightlife areas
- chill zones
- date-free social hangouts
- airport-area gems
- local tips
- traveler-approved places

Exact user GPS must never be public.

Sensitive Hidden Gems should support blurred or delayed location reveal.

Hidden Gems should be verified through a mix of:
- GPS proximity verification
- user submissions
- local guide approval
- community confirmation
- admin review
- Compass AI safety checks
- Pulse activity
- plan/check-in history

SENSITIVITY LEVELS

Support sensitivity levels:

1. Public
- Exact location can be shown.

2. Approximate
- Show neighborhood/area first.

3. Reveal after save
- User must save or join related plan before exact details show.

4. Reveal after acceptance
- Exact location only visible to accepted plan/trip members.

5. Protected
- Exact location hidden publicly to prevent overcrowding, abuse, or safety issues.

Use sensitivity for:
- small local spots
- fragile nature spots
- private-feeling locations
- places that may be unsafe alone
- locations reported for harassment/scams
- places that should not be overrun
