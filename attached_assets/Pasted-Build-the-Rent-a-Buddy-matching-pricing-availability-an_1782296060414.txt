Build the Rent a Buddy matching, pricing, availability, and marketplace layer.

Goal:
Make Rent a Buddy work like a real marketplace where travelers can find the right Buddy, request custom help, compare prices, book available Buddies, and where Buddies can earn through hourly, half-day, full-day, group, and package bookings.

Group bookings:
Support one Buddy for multiple travelers.

Rules:
- Buddy must be group-approved.
- Group size cannot exceed Buddy max_group_size.
- Public meetup required by default.
- Traveler who books is group lead.
- Optional split-pay support can be placeholder if payments are not ready.
- Group lead is responsible for booking deposit unless split-pay is built.
- Store guest count and guest names/display names if available.
