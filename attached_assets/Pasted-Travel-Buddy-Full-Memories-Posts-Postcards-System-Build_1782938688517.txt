Travel Buddy — Full Memories / Posts / Postcards System Buildout, Frontend + Backend

You are building the full Memories / Posts / Postcards system for Travel Buddy.

Important product direction:
Travel Buddy is a social media app for travelers. Posts are not generic social posts. They are travel memories: postcards, moments, videos, place recommendations, trip updates, event updates, hidden gems, city stories, and Passport-linked memories.

Design correction:
Do not copy Instagram’s post design. The goal is to make photos/postcards large, immersive, and social-media-friendly while still feeling unique to Travel Buddy.

Travel Buddy memories should feel like a mix of:
- travel postcard
- passport page
- city journal
- photo diary
- social feed
- trip memory card

Do not delete existing working systems.
Do not fake posts, users, comments, likes, views, shares, places, reviews, trips, events, bookings, or activity.
Inspect what exists first.
Patch/build only after inventory.
Preserve auth/session behavior.
Use real backend data.
Everything must be cohesive with Pulse, Discovery, Trips, Events, Passport/Profile, Telegraph, Circles, Rent a Buddy, Passport Stamps, Trust Score, verification, delayed posting, privacy, safety, hashtags, tagging, and location-aware discovery.

Primary goal:
Build a complete Memories/Post system where users can:
- create travel posts
- create postcard-style posts
- upload photos/videos
- add captions
- tag real places
- tag users
- add hashtags
- link posts to trips, events, circles, and Rent a Buddy bookings when allowed
- recommend places
- mark hidden gems
- control privacy
- use delayed posting for location safety
- like, comment, save, share, and report posts
- show posts in Pulse
- show posts on Passport/Profile
- show trip/event/city/place-linked memories
- earn Passport Stamps from real activity
- moderate unsafe content
- preserve user safety and location privacy
- display large immersive photo/postcard cards that feel uniquely Travel Buddy, not like an Instagram clone

PHASE 1 — Product rules

Memories / Posts must support several travel-native post types.

Post types:
- standard_post
- postcard
- photo_memory
- video_memory
- trip_update
- event_update
- place_recommendation
- hidden_gem
- review_like_memory
- city_story
- arrival_post
- departure_post
- rent_buddy_memory
- circle_post

Rules:
- No fake posts or fake engagement.
- Posts can be public, friends-only, followers-only, crew-only, circle-only, event-only, trip-only, private, draft, or delayed.
- Location-sensitive posts must support delayed publishing.
- User can earn geotagged/stamp credit immediately, but public visibility waits until delayed-posting safety rules pass.
- Precise location must never be exposed by default.
- Posts linked to private trips/events/circles must not leak private context.
- Deleted/removed posts should not remain publicly visible.
- Reported posts can be hidden or moderated based on severity.
- Blocked users cannot see or interact with each other’s posts.
- Comments/likes visibility must respect privacy settings.
- Users can hide likes and turn off comments.
- Users can choose who can comment and who can share when supported.
- Tagging users must respect tag permissions.
- Hashtags should be clickable and searchable.
- Large media layouts must obey the same privacy/delayed posting rules as compact layouts.
