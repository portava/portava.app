Create standalone Travel Buddy app at ~/workspace/travel-buddy-standalone.

Goal:
Physically separate Travel Buddy from the web-infra monorepo while keeping it inside the current Replit environment for now.

Reason:
A fully separate repo/Replit project is the cleanest final state, but it adds setup friction right now. ~/workspace/travel-buddy-standalone gives a safe transition path: separate app root, separate lockfile, separate EAS config, separate package-manager context, easier EAS build, still accessible inside the same Replit environment. Original artifacts/travel-buddy remains untouched as backup.

Do NOT use ~/travel-buddy-app — Replit path/environment constraints make a folder outside ~/workspace risky and outside the expected workspace tooling.

Physical target:
Create the standalone app at: ~/workspace/travel-buddy-standalone

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CRITICAL — standalone must NOT be a workspace member (read first)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
This is the whole point of the decouple. If the new folder is treated as a pnpm
workspace member, EAS will get confused exactly as before and the effort is wasted.

- Confirm the root pnpm-workspace.yaml packages globs (currently: artifacts/*, lib/*,
  scripts) do NOT match travel-buddy-standalone. It must NOT appear when running
  `pnpm -r list` from ~/workspace.
- If needed, explicitly exclude it (e.g. ensure no glob captures it). Do NOT add it to
  the workspace.
- The standalone needs its OWN pnpm-lock.yaml, generated by running `pnpm install`
  INSIDE ~/workspace/travel-buddy-standalone — NOT the root lockfile, NOT a symlink to it.
- Verify after install:
    ls ~/workspace/travel-buddy-standalone/pnpm-lock.yaml   (its own lockfile exists)
    ls ~/workspace/travel-buddy-standalone/node_modules     (its own node_modules, not root)
- The standalone package.json must have ZERO "workspace:*" deps and ZERO catalog: refs.
  (These were already cleaned in artifacts/travel-buddy — confirm the copied version
  carries the cleaned package.json, including tsx pinned to 4.22.4, not "catalog:".)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Hard rules
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
- Do not delete artifacts/travel-buddy.
- Do not mutate the original app destructively.
- Do not keep the standalone app dependent on the parent monorepo root.
