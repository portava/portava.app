Build the Travel Buddy Notifications and Real-Time Activity Center.

Build this on top of Trips, Plans, GPS/location, Discovery, City Pulse, Telegraph, Safe Return, Trip Crew, Compass AI, Passport Stamps, Hidden Gems, Local Guides, Airport/Layover Mode, Trust Score, and Admin moderation.

Stay focused on notifications, alerts, reminders, activity center, real-time updates, user preferences, and delivery rules.

Do not expand into unrelated new features in this pass.

CORE PRINCIPLE

Travel Buddy needs one unified notification and activity system that handles all user-facing alerts consistently.

Notifications should be useful, timely, privacy-safe, and not spammy.

Every major feature should send events through one shared notification pipeline instead of creating separate one-off notification logic.

NOTIFICATION PRIVACY RULES

1. Do not reveal exact GPS in notification previews.
2. Do not reveal hotel/home/private stay location.
3. Do not reveal private plan location to pending users.
4. Do not reveal Safe Return details to unauthorized users.
5. Do not expose live-share location in push preview.
6. Telegraph previews must respect privacy settings.
7. Trust notifications must not reveal reporter identity.
8. Admin/security alerts should be private to the user.
9. Removed users must not receive private trip/plan updates.
10. Ghost Mode must suppress location/status-based notifications to others.

Examples:

Bad:
- “Andre is at 123 Main Street.”
- “Maya is at her hotel.”
- “Private plan location: exact address.”

Good:
- “Andre shared live location with you.”
- “A crew member arrived near the venue.”
- “Your meetup location is now available in the app.”
