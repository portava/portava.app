Build the Travel Buddy “Rent a Buddy” system.

Goal:
Create a safe, verified local companion marketplace where travelers can book verified Buddies for city exploration, nightlife, language help, shopping, arrival support, content/photo help, group support, or custom local guidance.

Important positioning:
The feature name is “Rent a Buddy.”
This is NOT a dating, escort, adult, romantic, or illegal-service feature.
Add clear policy language anywhere users book or apply to become a Buddy:
“Rent a Buddy is for travel companionship, city guidance, local help, language support, and social exploration only. It is not a dating, escort, adult, romantic, or sexual service. Violations can lead to account review, suspension, or removal.”

Core user roles:
1. Traveler — books a Buddy.
2. Buddy — verified user offering local companionship/guidance.
3. Admin — reviews Buddies, handles disputes, manages flags.

Build these booking categories:
- City Buddy: landmarks, food, markets, hidden gems, walking around.
- Nightlife Buddy: bars, lounges, clubs, party areas.
- Language Buddy: translation and local help.
- Shopping Buddy: malls, fashion districts, local markets.
- Arrival Buddy: airport/city setup, SIM card, hotel check-in, transport help.
- Content Buddy: photos/videos around the city.
- Group Buddy: one Buddy helping a small group.
- Custom Buddy: traveler describes what they need.

Payment rules:
Support two payment options:
1. Deposit in app + cash balance due at meetup.
2. Full payment in app.

Rules:
- Booking is not confirmed until deposit or full payment is paid in app.
- Cash balance must be displayed clearly before confirmation.
- Buddy cannot request more money outside the app.
- Traveler cannot request unpaid extra time.
- Extra time must be added and confirmed through the app.
- Off-app booking/payment avoidance should trigger warnings and Trust Score penalties.

Example checkout text:
Total price: $100
Paid today: $25 deposit
Cash due at meetup: $75
Travel Buddy service fee: $5
Booking is confirmed after deposit payment.

Extra time:
Add “Add Time” flow:
- Traveler or Buddy can request extra time.
- Choose 30 min, 1 hour, 2 hours, or custom.
- Show added price.
- Choose payment mode: in-app or cash balance.
- Both users must confirm before extension starts.
- Store extension records.

During booking, if Buddy wants to change the route, require traveler approval.
Store route change request and response.

Messaging:
Create or connect a temporary Telegraph thread for each confirmed Rent a Buddy booking.
Thread should include traveler and Buddy.
Thread can archive after booking completion unless both users choose to stay connected.
Use existing Telegraph translation system if available.

Rules and policy enforcement:
Flag messages or booking requests containing banned intent:
- sexual services
- escort
- girlfriend/boyfriend experience
- massage/adult services
- drugs
- illegal activity
- off-app payment
- private hotel room/private home as first meetup
- harassment
- threats

Do not overbuild AI moderation if not already available. Start with keyword/rule-based flags and admin review queue.

Reviews:
After completion, both sides review each other.
Use double-blind reviews: hide reviews until both submit or review window closes.

Review categories:
- On time
- Respectful
- Helpful
- Local knowledge
- Communication
- Safety
- Matched profile
- Worth the price
- Would book again

Also allow private notes visible only to admin/safety system.

Trust Score:
Rent a Buddy should affect Trust Score.

Buddy gains score for:
- Completed booking
- On-time arrival
- Positive reviews
- Safe Return completed
- No reports
- Repeat booking
- Staying on-app
- Confirmed cash balance properly

Buddy loses score for:
- Late cancellation
- No-show
- Off-app payment request
- Unsafe route change
- Inappropriate request
- Harassment
- Misleading profile
- Confirmed safety report
- Abandoning traveler

Traveler gains score for:
- Showing up on time
- Paying cash balance confirmed
- Respectful review
- No reports
- Staying on-app

Traveler loses score for:
- No-show
- Refusing agreed cash balance
- Harassment
- Sexual/romantic/adult-service request
- Illegal request
- Bringing unapproved people
- Off-app pressure
- Threatening bad reviews

Category-specific approval:
A Buddy can be approved for some categories but not others.
Example:
Approved: City Buddy, Food/Shopping Buddy
Not approved: Nightlife Buddy, Arrival Buddy

New Buddy limits:
For new Buddies, apply default restrictions:
- Public meetup required
- Daytime only unless manually approved
- Max 2-hour booking
- No group bookings until trusted
- No nightlife until approved
- Full in-app payment or higher deposit required if risk rules require it

Acceptance criteria:
- Rent a Buddy exists as a full feature area.
- Travelers can search, view, request, pay deposit/full payment, and manage bookings.
- Buddies can apply, manage profile/availability, accept/decline requests, and see earnings.
- Admin can review applications and handle disputes/flags.
- Clear non-dating/non-adult-service policy appears in the application and booking flow.
- Deposit + cash balance and full in-app payment are both supported.
- Safety tools are available during active bookings.
- Booking status is honest and never claims completion before both sides complete or admin resolves.
- All new code is tested and does not break existing Travel Buddy features.
