Run a follow-up Travel Buddy audit focused on unresolved items, partial repairs, regressions, and anything still not fully connected after the first full-system audit.

This is not a fresh rebuild. This is a strict verification + repair follow-up pass to make sure nothing remains half-wired, misleading, or broken.

GOAL:
Close all gaps left after the main audit. Confirm that every repaired system is truly connected end-to-end, that no regressions were introduced, and that no feature remains partially implemented or disconnected from the rest of the app.

IMPORTANT RULES:
- First inspect the results of the previous audit and repair pass.
- Reuse the previous audit report, connectivity matrix, broken path report, source-of-truth map, and manual QA notes.
- Focus on unresolved items, partial items, regressions, weak spots, and anything marked follow-up.
- Do not rebuild working systems unnecessarily.
- Do not create fake data.
- Do not mark anything complete unless the frontend reflects real backend truth.
- If a button or screen still exists, it must work, be honestly disabled, or be removed.

9. Admin/internal follow-up
- admin actions not reflected in normal UI
